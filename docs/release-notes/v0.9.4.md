### Fixed
- The app's own labels (the counter overlay text, the game name, "Deaths") are never taken for a boss name, even when they show up elsewhere on screen, such as in OBS's preview.
- Dark Souls Remastered has its own boss-name region at the left end of the boss bar, so item names like "Divine Blessing" are no longer read as bosses.

### Added
- `ocr-misses.log` in the data folder: each time you press F9 because a death was missed, the app writes what OCR read in the 10 seconds before. Send it with a bug report.

Wrong boss entries saved by earlier versions stay in the Boss tab: remove them with **Xóa** or move their deaths with **Gộp vào...**.
