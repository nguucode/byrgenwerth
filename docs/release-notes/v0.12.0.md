A smaller window, a label of your choice next to the count, and Vietnamese narration of the translation. Windows only from now on.

```powershell
irm https://raw.githubusercontent.com/nguucode/byrgenwerth/main/install.ps1 | iex
```

### New
- **Vietnamese narration** (Translate → Thuyết minh): press a key (default Ctrl+Alt+V) or a gamepad button combination to hear the translation, with a female or male voice. It runs on your PC with Piper, downloaded once with the **Tải** button (about 85 MB for the female voice, 50 MB for the male one).
- **Label next to the count**: a word or your own PNG/SVG picture, in one of the four corners around the number, with its own size. Shown on the overlay and in OBS.

### Changed
- Redesigned, more compact window: tabs Nhận diện, Hiển thị, Overlay/OBS, Cài đặt; Translate has Dịch and Thuyết minh. Inputs and buttons share one size, everything is aligned left, longer explanations moved into tooltips.
- Death detection works the same way for every game (OCR plus learned templates). The list of per-game presets is gone; your keywords are kept.
- New games start at a 70% template threshold (now a number box) and a 10 s cooldown. Existing games keep theirs.
- Rename and delete a game are in the Cài đặt tab.
- The OBS text file now holds the label word and the count. Your old display format becomes the label word, e.g. "Deaths: {count}" → "Deaths".
- Set the OBS Browser Source height to 400 so a label above or below the count fits.

### Removed
- The macOS version.
- Counting by the health bar ("Máu nhân vật"). Press F9 in games without a death screen.
